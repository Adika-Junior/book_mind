    Research checklist
    1. Start with the primary source. Read the Bill's clause itself; use the Digest and Strategy to understand it, not to replace it.
    2. Cite as you go. Keep the page (e.g. [Bill, p.9]) with every point — answers here do this for you.
    3. Write it in your own words. Add Your note to anything you save; that's where understanding happens.
    4. Check sources beyond the documents with SIFT: Stop · Investigate the source · Find better coverage · Trace claims to the original.
    5. Read laterally. For an unfamiliar website, open other tabs to see who is behind it before reading it closely. Prefer official (.go.ke, Kenya Law) and intergovernmental sources.
    6. Mind the date. The Bill is a 2026 draft — note when you accessed anything and whether it may be out of date.
    7. One session per question. Export the session as a brief and tick its verification checklist before sharing.
